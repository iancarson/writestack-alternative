---

## Why People Look for WriteStack Alternatives

WriteStack pioneered third-party Substack Notes scheduling and still has an active user base. Common reasons writers look for alternatives:

- **Browser dependency:** WriteStack is a Chrome extension. Your browser must be open at publish time. If your computer sleeps or Chrome crashes, scheduled Notes don't publish
- **Notes-only:** WriteStack doesn't cross-post to LinkedIn, Medium, or X
- **Scheduling cap:** The base plan limits you to 20 scheduled Notes at a time
- **No cross-platform analytics:** You can't track which Notes are driving Substack subscriber growth vs. just measuring restacks
- **Single platform:** For writers active on LinkedIn, Medium, or X, WriteStack only solves one piece of the workflow

## Who Should Use Each Tool

### Use Native Substack (Free) If:
- You only publish to Substack — no LinkedIn, Medium, or X cross-posting
- You schedule fewer than 10 Notes per month
- You don't need batch import or analytics
- Cost is the primary concern

### Use WriteStack (~$24/mo) If:
- You need more scheduling flexibility than native Substack (more slots, longer horizon)
- You're comfortable leaving Chrome open at publish time
- You don't need cross-posting to other platforms
- You prefer a writing-focused interface

### Use StackSweller (~$25/mo) If:
- You want cloud-based Notes scheduling (no browser dependency)
- You want Notes analytics without cross-posting
- Your entire distribution is Substack-only
- The additional $1/mo over WriteStack is worth no-browser-dependency to you

### Use Narrareach ($39/mo) If:
- You cross-post (or want to cross-post) to LinkedIn, Medium, and/or X
- You want unlimited batch scheduling from one calendar
- You want analytics that tracks which Notes and posts drove new Substack subscribers
- You need Medium publishing without depending on new API integration tokens
- You want a tool that grows with your distribution strategy, not just a scheduler

→ [Start Narrareach 7-day free trial](https://narrareach.com/vs/writestack)

### Use StackBuddy (~$19/mo) If:
- You want a lower-cost cloud-based option
- Notes-only scheduling is sufficient
- You're evaluating all options before committing

## The Fundamental Split: Scheduler vs. Distribution Platform

WriteStack, StackSweller, StackBuddy, and the Chrome extensions are all **Notes schedulers** — they do one thing: help you schedule Substack Notes on a timeline.

[Narrareach](https://narrareach.com/vs/writestack) is a **distribution platform** — it schedules Notes AND automatically distributes your content to LinkedIn, Medium, and X. For writers whose growth strategy includes platforms beyond Substack, this isn't a WriteStack comparison. They're solving different problems.

If your goal is:
- Scheduling Notes on Substack → WriteStack or StackSweller
- Reaching LinkedIn + Medium + X audiences automatically → Narrareach

## FAQ

### Is Narrareach worth $15 more per month than WriteStack?

The $15/mo delta covers: cloud-based publishing (no browser dependency), automatic LinkedIn cross-posting, Medium publishing (post-API), X cross-posting, unlimited scheduling, cross-platform analytics, and AI writing tools. For writers actively growing on multiple platforms, the ROI on the additional cost is typically positive within 2–3 months of new subscriber acquisition.

### Does WriteStack still work in 2026?

Yes — WriteStack is actively maintained. The core limitations remain: Chrome extension architecture (browser must be open), Notes-only publishing, 20-Note base plan cap, no cross-platform analytics.

### What happened to the WriteStack free tier?

WriteStack has historically not offered a free tier. Check their current pricing at writestack.io for the most up-to-date plans.

### Can I use both WriteStack and Narrareach?

Technically yes, but practically there's no reason to. Both handle Substack Notes scheduling. Choose based on whether cross-posting matters to your workflow.

### Are there any free Substack Notes schedulers?

Native Substack scheduling is free and supports scheduling up to 30 days ahead. It's the only free option. All third-party tools are paid.
