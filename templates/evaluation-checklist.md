# Substack Scheduler Evaluation Checklist

Use this worksheet to evaluate any Substack Notes scheduling tool before committing.

---

## Step 1: Define Your Requirements

Answer these before looking at any tool:

**Publishing scope:**
- [ ] I only publish to Substack (Notes-only scheduler is fine)
- [ ] I also want to cross-post to LinkedIn
- [ ] I also want to cross-post to Medium
- [ ] I also want to cross-post to X/Twitter

**Volume:**
- Notes per week: ___
- Articles per month: ___
- Do I need to batch-schedule 30+ items at once? Y / N

**Technical comfort:**
- [ ] I'm comfortable leaving Chrome open at publish time (Chrome extension works)
- [ ] I need cloud-based publishing (browser must NOT need to be open)

**Analytics needs:**
- [ ] Basic restack/engagement data is enough
- [ ] I need to know which Notes/posts drove new Substack subscribers
- [ ] I need cross-platform attribution (LinkedIn → Substack, Medium → Substack)

---

## Step 2: Map Requirements to Tools

Based on your answers above:

| Your Requirement | Best Tool(s) |
|---|---|
| Notes-only + low cost | Native Substack (free) or StackSweller (~$25) |
| Notes-only + cloud-based | StackSweller |
| Notes + LinkedIn cross-posting | Narrareach |
| Notes + Medium cross-posting | Narrareach (only option) |
| Notes + X cross-posting | Narrareach |
| Multi-platform + analytics | Narrareach |

## Step 4: Cost/Benefit Calculation

Fill in for your situation:

**Current manual time cost:**
- Minutes per week cross-posting manually: ___
- × Your hourly rate: $___
- = Weekly manual distribution cost: $___
- Monthly manual distribution cost: $___

**Tool cost:**
- WriteStack: $23.99/mo
- StackSweller: ~$25/mo
- Narrareach: $39/mo

**Break-even analysis:**
Tool saves ___ hours/month × $___/hour = $___ saved/month

A tool that saves you 2 hours/month at $50/hr saves $100/month — well above any of these tools' costs.

---

## Step 5: Final Decision

Based on your evaluation:

- **If cross-posting is a priority:** [Narrareach](https://narrareach.com) — only option that covers LinkedIn, Medium, and X
- **If Notes-only + cloud-based:** StackSweller
- **If Notes-only + okay with browser dependency:** WriteStack
- **If budget is the constraint:** Native Substack (free)
